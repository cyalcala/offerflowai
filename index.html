The Roadmap

A simple journey from unclear offer to usable video system.

A completely productized process designed for speed, clarity, and zero friction. Here is exactly what happens once you request a pack.

01

Offer Intake

You submit your offer details, target audience, primary call-to-action (CTA), current content samples, and brand voice guidelines via a simple form.

02

Angle Map

I map your core offer into key structural communication angles: hooks, client pain points, desired transformation outcomes, objections, proof vectors, and conversion CTAs.

03

Script Pack

I write 5 short, high-impact scripts designed specifically for positioning clarity, avoiding vague agency fluff and generic social media padding.

04

AI UGC Production

I produce short AI-assisted UGC videos using premium visual presenters and layouts, based on the approved scripting mapping and voice references.

05

Posting Sequence

You receive the final, high-definition videos along with a recommended tactical posting and testing sequence optimized for your target platforms.

06

Optional Follow-Up Flow

For businesses seeking the full conversion system, we connect these assets to dedicated landing pages, opt-in CTAs, email follow-up flows, or GoHighLevel automation rules.
